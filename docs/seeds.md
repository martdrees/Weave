# Seeds

Seeds are tracks you mark as references for **Recommendations** and **Generator**.

![Toolbar shows a seed count when seeds are marked](screenshots/toolbar.png)

When seeds are active, the toolbar shows a badge such as `3 seeds`.

## Mark or unmark a seed

1. Load a playlist.
2. Click the track row (seed control / row affordance) to **mark as seed**.
3. Click again to **remove seed**.

## Why seeds matter


| Feature                               | Without seeds                           | With seeds                                                         |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| [Recommendations](recommendations.md) | Uses the whole playlist as soft context | Prefer seed audio features; **Fit to seeds** sets ranges from them |
| [Generator](generator.md)             | Soft-seeds from the playlist            | Generates from marked seed track IDs                               |




## Tips

- Mark 1–5 varied seeds for better radio-style results.
- Seeds are part of the session; they are not a separate Spotify playlist.
